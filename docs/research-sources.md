## Primary sources

- **NEO at scale (2026):** [AIP publisher record](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/aco/article/2/2/026109/3394290/Nuclear-electronic-orbital-method-at-scale-A-two), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0318199). Title, authors, publication status, bibliographic details and summary verified. The large-cluster timing in the abstract refers to one Fock build, not a complete simulation; no headline benchmark number is used.
- **Two-spinor Ehrenfest (2026):** [RSC article](https://doi.org/10.1039/D6CP02263D). The reported fields are much stronger than typical experimental fields, so the summary labels these as proof-of-concept simulations.
- **Strong-field ionization (2026):** [AIP publisher record](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/165/1/014105/3397038/Spin-orbit-resolved-strong-field-ionization-from), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0320154). Summary based on the publisher abstract.
- **Vibrational spectroscopy (2026):** [AIP publisher record](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/164/14/144123/3386961/Nuclear-electronic-orbital-quasiclassical), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0317500). Summary based on the publisher abstract. Protons are treated quantum mechanically; remaining nuclei follow quasiclassical initial conditions and Ehrenfest trajectories, rather than a fully quantum treatment of every nucleus.
- **Magnetic quantum dynamics (2025):** [ACS publisher record](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c00273). Title, all five authors, equal contribution, citation and summary verified.
- **GPU relativistic DFT (2024):** [ACS publisher record](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c00843). The summary accurately identifies acceleration of the numerical integration step; no universal end-to-end speedup claim is made.
