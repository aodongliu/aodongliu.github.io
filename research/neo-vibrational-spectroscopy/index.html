Vibrational spectroscopy / 2026

Predicting molecular vibrations beyond simple springs

Molecular vibrations become difficult to predict when atoms move far from the simple spring-like picture. This approach combines quantum protons with carefully initialized trajectories for other nuclei to capture more realistic vibrational spectra.
