GPU acceleration / 2024

Using GPUs to accelerate heavy-element simulations

Accurate simulations of heavy elements must account for relativistic effects, adding computational cost. This work moves a major numerical integration step onto GPUs and benchmarks the improvement on copper, silver, and gold clusters.
