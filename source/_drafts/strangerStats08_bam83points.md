On **March 10, 2026**, Bam Adebayo dropped **83 points** on the Washington Wizards, the **second-most in NBA history** behind only Wilt Chamberlain's 100 and ahead of Kobe Bryant's 81. He shot 20-of-43, knocked down 7 threes, and made a ridiculous **36 free throws**.

So it's the greatest game ever played, right?

In daily fantasy? It's **fifth**. And the four games ahead of it will make you do a double-take, because in DFS scoring a point is worth one point while **a block and a steal are worth three**.

Here's the FanDuel formula (the one StatMuse ranks by):

**FanDuel = PTS + 1.2×REB + 1.5×AST + 3×STL + 3×BLK − TOV**

Run Bam's 83 through it:

83 points → 83. 9 rebounds → 10.8. 3 assists → 4.5. 2 steals → 6. 2 blocks → 6. 5 turnovers → −5. Total: **105.3**, the 5th-best fantasy game ever.

Look at what's beating 83 points. **A 38-point game with 12 blocks. A 69-point game. Another Hakeem game with 11 blocks. And Shaq with 24 points and 15 blocks.**

Yes, you read that last one right. **Shaq's 24-point game out-scored Bam's 83-point game** (107.1 vs 105.3). Shaq's 15 blocks were worth **45 fantasy points**, and his 28 rebounds another 33.6. Bam's 83 points were worth 83, but the rest of his line (9 rebounds, 3 assists, 2 steals, 2 blocks) barely moved the needle.

And the #1 game ever? Hakeem's 12 blocks plus 7 steals produced **57 fantasy points on defense alone**, more than his 38 points. Defense is where daily-fantasy money lives.

## Who Owns the Most Top-100 Games?

One monster game is a fun night. The real question is who keeps showing up in the top 100, over and over. So I counted every player's appearances in the all-time top 100:

{% htmlblock p08/top100_occurrence %}

**Hakeem Olajuwon owns 16 of the top 100 fantasy games ever.** Sixteen. That's more than Anthony Davis (7) and Nikola Jokic (6) combined. He's #1, #3, and #8 on the all-time list, plus thirteen more scattered through the top 100. In a scoring system that pays three times for a block, the greatest shot-blocker of all time is the greatest fantasy player of all time, and it isn't close.

## What We Learned

**Bam scored the 2nd-most points ever, and it's the 5th-best fantasy game ever.** Both are true, and they don't contradict each other.

Points are the cheapest stat in daily fantasy. A block pays three times a point, a steal pays three times, a rebound pays 1.2 times. That's why three of the top four fantasy games ever are defensive block-fests, why an 83-point scoring binge can't crack the top four, and why Hakeem owns more of the leaderboard than anyone else.

So no shade on Bam. 83 is a night most players dream about. But in the scoring system that actually runs daily fantasy, swatting a shot pays better than making one.
