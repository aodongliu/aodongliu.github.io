After last season ended I saw [this post from @NBAcrazystats](https://x.com/NBAcrazystats/status/2106711850423627949): **DeMar DeRozan** is now top 10 all-time in games lost.

A six-time All-Star, on a list like that? I had to look. And once I was looking: who has lost the most games in NBA history, and who has the worst plus-minus?

---

### DeMar DeRozan

{% htmlblock p10/demar_derozan %}

**634 losses**, tied for 10th all-time.

The strange part is how he got there. Going into last season he was **610-577**, 33 games over .500. Then he went **20-57** with the Kings, with a **−588** plus-minus that is the 25th-worst season on record.

One year took him from a winning career to a losing one, and from +204 to −384.

### The Losses List

{% htmlblock p10/most_losses_table %}

{% htmlblock p10/vince_carter %}

**Vince Carter** lost **756 games**. He also won 785 and finished his career at +1,817.

That's what this list really measures. Half of the top 12 won more than they lost, and **Kevin Garnett** is 9th with an MVP and a ring. You need 15+ years of teams wanting you to lose 600 games. Count the playoffs too and **LeBron James** enters the top 10, while DeRozan drops to 16th.

So to find the players who really lost, I need a rate.

### Lowest Win Rate

{% htmlblock p10/lowest_win_pct_table %}

{% htmlblock p10/shareef_abdur_rahim %}

**Shareef Abdur-Rahim** went **275-555**. He lost two of every three games for 12 years while averaging **18.1 points**. His first five seasons were with the Vancouver Grizzlies, who never won more than 23 games with him.

He played **744 games** before his first playoff game, in 2006. The team that finally got him there: the Sacramento Kings.

Lower the bar to 500 games and his Vancouver teammate **Doug West** is the leader at 195-480 (.289). Another teammate, **Bryant Reeves**, spent his whole career there and went **87-308**.

### Worst Career Plus-Minus

Plus-minus has been tracked since 1996-97, which happens to be Abdur-Rahim's rookie year.

{% htmlblock p10/worst_career_plus_minus_table %}

Him again, by more than 550 points. Two different questions, same answer.

**Jeff Green** is 8th here and 14th in losses. **Juwan Howard** is 10th here and tied with DeRozan at 634 losses. They have three rings between them.

DeRozan's −384 is around **460th** from the bottom, out of 2,856 players. He lost for a long time. He didn't lose badly.

### Bub Carrington

{% htmlblock p10/bub_carrington %}

Whaaaaat????

The worst season on record as a rookie, then almost the same thing again. He played every game for a Wizards team that won 18 and then 17. After **164 games** he is already in the bottom 25 for a career, next to players who needed 800.

{% htmlblock p10/worst_season_plus_minus_table %}

Before anyone gives up on him, look at #8. That 19-year-old **Kevin Durant** is now at **+5,073**, 8th-best on record.

### One Night in Utah

{% htmlblock p10/cody_williams %}

The Jazz lost to the Hornets by 55 at home, and **Cody Williams** was a **−60**. The old record was −58, shared by Scoot Henderson and Jeremiah Robinson-Earl.

Utah won the 16 minutes he sat by 5.

---

For the record, the other end of both lists is **Tim Duncan**: 1,001-391 and **+8,910**.

DeRozan needs **122** more losses to tie Carter. At last season's pace, that's a little over two years.
