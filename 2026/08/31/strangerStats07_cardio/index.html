    Stranger Stats #7: Out There Doing Cardio, 20 Minutes of NBA Basketball With Nothing to Show for It

    September 14, 2026 · Aodong Liu

    • Stranger Stats
    • nba
    • data
    • basketball

    You ever watch a game and see a guy out there running around for 20+ minutes? Clapping his hands, calling for the ball, playing hard defense… then you check the box score and it’s 0 points, 0 rebounds, 0 assists, 0 steals, 0 blocks? Just absolutely nothing in every column?

    If you are like me, you are picturing Tony Snell, right?

    I call it a cardio game. Just out there running suicides basically. If you are like me, you are picturing Tony Snell, right? He is the poster child for this type of games. Well it turns out it happens to plenty of other players too. Let’s find these guys.

    By the Numbers

    Since the NBA started tracking steals and blocks in the 1973-74 season, there have been 31 games where a player logged 20+ minutes with 0/0/0/0/0:

    • 25 regular season games · 6 playoff games
    • 6 games at the 25+ minute mark.
    • 1 player has done it twice: Bruce Bowen (more on him later 👀)

    If we count the pre-1974 era (where steals/blocks weren’t tracked yet), there are 8 more games, including Andy Phillip playing 33 minutes with zero across the board in 1956. But let not count those here.

    The Two-Time Champ: Bruce Bowen 👑

    There’s only one player in NBA history who has pulled off the cardio game multiple times in the modern era. Bruce Bowen: 8-time All-Defensive selection, 3-time NBA champion, and apparently, a cardio enthusiast.

    Game 1 · 2005 Regular Season · 25 minutes

    Bruce Bowen

    Nov 5, 2005 · San Antonio Spurs @ Dallas Mavericks · L

    A routine cardio performance in November 2005: 25 minutes, 0-for-4 from the field, 2 fouls, 1 turnover, -12. Nothing special by cardio standards. But Bruce wasn’t done.

    Game 2 · 2008 Playoffs · 20 minutes

    Bruce Bowen

    Apr 27, 2008 · San Antonio Spurs @ Phoenix Suns · L

    0 PTS · 0 REB · 0 AST · 0 STL · 0 BLK

    Playoffs · 20 MIN

    • MIN 20
    • FG 0/3
    • FT 0/0
    • PF 1
    • TOV 0

    This is the one that makes him a legend. 2008 playoffs, Spurs at Phoenix, first round. Bruce Bowen plays 20 minutes, goes 0-for-3, and posts a -33 plus-minus. Minus thirty-three! In 20 minutes! The Spurs lost by 19 and Bowen’s -33 was by far the worst mark on the team. When your box score says 0/0/0/0/0 and your +/- is -33, you were not having a good day.

    Two cardio games, one in the playoffs, and the worst plus-minus on the entire list. Bruce Bowen: the only man to visit the cardio club twice, and he made the second visit count.
