    Stranger Stats #9: No Field Goals, All Free Throws. Who's Been Carried by the Refs?

    September 28, 2026 · Aodong Liu

    • Stranger Stats
    • nba
    • data
    • basketball

    Cover photo: NBA.com

    I’ve been watching AJ Dybantsa’s summer league games, and I have complicated feelings.

    The kid is special. The athleticism jumps off the screen, the tools are all there, and you can see exactly why everyone’s been hyping him as a franchise-changer. Genuinely exciting to watch a prospect like that get his first real run.

    And then he does the thing. He drives, throws his arms up, snaps his head back like he just got shot, and looks straight at the ref. Over and over. He’s 19 and he’s already got the flop-and-sell routine down to muscle memory.

    I get it. He watched the last ten years of NBA basketball and figured out where the game is going. Draw the foul, get to the line, let the refs put points on the board for you. It’s smart. It’s the meta. But man, it is frustrating to watch. Foul baiting, the exaggerated simulation, the head snap. It’s the part of modern basketball I like the least, and now the No. 1 prospect in the country is doing it before he’s even played an NBA minute.

    Which got me thinking about the guy who turned this into an entire identity. The patron saint of getting carried by the refs.

    James Harden. On December 26, 2013, he scored 27 points on 2 made field goals.

    So how far does this go? If you make zero shots, or one, or two, what’s the most points anyone has ever scored? And who lived at the line for a whole season?

    The Ceiling

    I went through every regular season and playoff box score since 1946 and found the record for each number of made shots:

    • 0 makes: 15 points
    • 1 make: 22 points
    • 2 makes: 27 points
    • 3 makes: 27 points

    Kevin Martin is next with 20, and his one make was a three.

    Charles Barkley almost got there first. In 1995 he scored 26 on 2-for-16 in 47 minutes. Fourteen misses, still 26 points.

    Jimmy Butler, A Hidden Free Throw Merchant

    Jimmy Butler has the reputation of one of the toughest guys in the league. Heat Culture, Playoff Jimmy, all of that. But anyone who’s had him on a fantasy team knows where his points come from.

    The single games: he scored 20+ points on 3 or fewer made shots three times, all within five weeks (November 20 to December 27, 2019).

    The seasons: that same 2019-20 is #5 on the list, with 37.9% of his points from the line. And it wasn’t a one-off. Out of 3,572 qualifying seasons, Butler has 6 in the top 70, five of them in Miami.

    Tough guy? Maybe. Free throw merchant? Definitely.

    If there’s a real patron saint of getting carried by the refs, it’s probably Corey Maggette. Jimmy is a close second.
